# Portfolio Index

## Purpose

The portfolio view prevents the database from becoming an undifferentiated list of ideas.

## Decision states

- **Selected** — receives focused investment.
- **Active** — currently being researched or validated.
- **Deferred** — plausible but not now.
- **Rejected** — evidence or constraints do not justify further work.
- **Superseded** — replaced by a better formulation.
- **Completed** — reached a reviewable outcome.

## Review questions

- What changed since the last review?
- Which assumption is carrying the model?
- What is the cheapest credible next test?
- What evidence would stop this work?
- Does it improve the Person Atlas, the Opportunity World, or both?
- Is the next commitment reversible?

## Portfolio metrics

Evidence strength, validation cost, strategic fit, access, learning value, economic potential, risk, recency, and unresolved uncertainty.
