# Opp Atlas Ultimate

## Purpose

Opp Atlas Ultimate is an evidence-backed opportunity database. It models opportunities as relationships between problems, people, capabilities, workflows, industries, technologies, constraints, evidence, experiments, and decisions.

## North Star

> Identify viable intersections between a person's evolving capabilities and an evolving economic opportunity space, then convert promising intersections into testable capability-acquisition or execution paths.

## Core loop

`Observe → Normalize → Research → Formulate → Evaluate → Validate → Decide → Learn`
