# Evaluation and Validation Prompt Kit

For each opportunity, identify the binding assumption and design the smallest credible experiment.

Return:

- opportunity thesis;
- assumption under test;
- evidence currently held;
- missing evidence;
- test method;
- sample or access route;
- success signal;
- kill signal;
- cost and time;
- decision boundary;
- database update.

Then perform an adversarial review: why might nobody pay, why might the problem not matter, why might existing solutions be adequate, and what hidden operational or credential constraint has been missed?
