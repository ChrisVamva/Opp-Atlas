# Opportunity Discovery Prompt Kit

## Core prompt

Act as an opportunity-discovery agent. First orient to the person's capabilities, constraints, access, time horizon, and goals. Then expand using independent generators: problem mining, capability arbitrage, workflow archaeology, technological discontinuity, cross-domain transfer, constraint exploitation, neglected markets, and combinatorial synthesis.

Do not converge early or produce generic startup ideas. After expansion, mutate ideas, filter them into KEEP / INVESTIGATE / PARK / DISCARD, create research questions, and return to brainstorming after research.

## Output

Opportunity spaces, raw ideas, mutations, research discoveries, second-generation ideas, unresolved questions, candidates, rejected ideas, and next highest-information question.
