# Synthesis and Decision Prompt Kit

Transform research and experiments into a decision-ready opportunity record.

Return:

1. concise thesis;
2. problem and affected actor;
3. current alternative;
4. opportunity mechanism;
5. evidence and confidence;
6. strongest contradiction;
7. capability requirements and person intersection;
8. risks and binding constraint;
9. reversible next action;
10. decision: select, research, validate, defer, reject, or reformulate;
11. provenance and unresolved questions.

Do not write a polished business plan when the evidence supports only a hypothesis.
