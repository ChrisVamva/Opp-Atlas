# Workflow and Problem Mining Protocol

Look for:

- repeated manual work;
- copied information;
- failed handoffs;
- spreadsheet dependence;
- coordination delays;
- rework, downtime, and billing delays;
- tasks dependent on one knowledgeable person;
- compliance burdens and new reporting obligations;
- strange practices and obsolete tools;
- problems between organizations rather than inside one organization.

Represent a workflow as:

`Input → Decision → Action → Handoff → Record → Verification → Output`

Capture observations before naming problems. Then connect repeated observations to actors, industries, workflows, constraints, and possible opportunities.
