# Opportunity Research Protocol

1. Define the opportunity and the decision it may change.
2. Identify the problem, affected actor, workflow, and current alternative.
3. Generate foundational, primary-source, implementation, trade-off, governance, and counterargument queries.
4. Prefer official documentation, standards, primary research, job descriptions, procurement material, complaints, reviews, and observed workarounds.
5. Separate facts from interpretation.
6. Search for disconfirming evidence.
7. Return to brainstorming after research to identify newly visible opportunity spaces.
8. Record the smallest credible next experiment.

Research should change the model, not merely decorate an existing idea.
