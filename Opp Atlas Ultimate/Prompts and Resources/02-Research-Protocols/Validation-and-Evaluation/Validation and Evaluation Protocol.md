# Validation and Evaluation Protocol

For each candidate, state:

- the highest-impact assumption;
- what evidence would support it;
- what evidence would weaken or kill it;
- the cheapest credible test;
- the observable success signal;
- the decision boundary;
- the next action if supported;
- the update if weakened.

Evaluate only after divergent discovery and research. Use fit, access, value, effort, risk, learning value, and evidence strength. Never let enthusiasm substitute for evidence.
