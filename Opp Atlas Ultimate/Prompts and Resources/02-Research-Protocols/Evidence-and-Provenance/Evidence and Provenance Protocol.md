# Evidence and Provenance Protocol

For every meaningful claim, record:

- claim text;
- claim type: fact, source-based claim, inference, assumption, or unknown;
- exact passage or precise paraphrase;
- source URL or locator;
- source date/version;
- evidence type;
- confidence;
- limitations;
- contradicting evidence;
- affected entity or opportunity.

Do not turn a vendor statement into a universal fact. Do not smooth disagreements away. Mark unsupported conclusions as hypotheses. Every synthesis should make clear what is observed, inferred, recommended, or still unknown.
