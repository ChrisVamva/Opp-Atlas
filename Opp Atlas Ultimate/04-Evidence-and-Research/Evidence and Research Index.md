# Evidence and Research Index

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Direct observation or interview
2. Primary source
3. Repeated independent sources
4. Secondary synthesis
5. Inference
6. Unchecked assumption

## Core records

- source;
- observation;
- claim;
- contradiction;
- research question;
- synthesis;
- provenance link.

## Research protocol

For each opportunity, research the smallest unknown that could change the decision. Separate fact, claim, inference, assumption, and unknown. Search for disconfirming evidence deliberately.

## Evidence ledger fields

`claim → claim_type → source → source_type → confidence → contradiction_status → related_opportunity → last_verified`

## Output

Research should create or reformulate opportunities, not only decorate existing ideas.
