## Important distinction

A raw idea belongs in discovery notes. A database opportunity requires a problem, context, mechanism, evidence state, uncertainty, and next validation step.

## Minimum opportunity graph

`Actor → Problem → Workflow → Industry → Opportunity → Required Capability → Evidence → Experiment`
