## Part C — Hands-on practical (20 pts)

*Use the lesson's cheat sheet only. Browser + a doc.*

Pick a topic you know (not tomatoes unless you genuinely grow them). Then:

1. **Research (8 min):** use autocomplete + People also ask + Related searches to collect **8 keywords**; label each informational or transactional.
2. **Choose (4 min):** select one *long-tail* keyword and check the SERP: who ranks — big brands or small focused sites? Record your evidence.
3. **Optimize (8 min):** for your keyword, write: title tag (≤60 chars, keyword early), meta description (~150 chars, with a reason to click), H1, and four H2s in a logical order.

**Extension (3 pts):** find one keyword where the SERP is *all videos* — write one sentence on what that tells you about intent (and what format you'd have to make).

**D3.** *(Bonus scenario, not scored separately)*: a page about growing tomatoes in pots keeps ranking for "best tomato fertilizer" but not the owner's main keyword — what does the SERP tell you to check?

## Answer key

<details><summary><strong>Part A</strong></summary>

**A1.** Crawl → index → rank. · **A2.** What the searcher *wants*; informational / navigational / transactional. · **A3.** Longer, specific, lower-competition phrases with clearer intent — realistic wins for new sites. · **A4.** It's your ad copy on the SERP: it decides whether searchers click (and engagement matters), even if it doesn't directly move rankings.
</details>

<details><summary><strong>Part B</strong></summary>

**B1.** title; 60 · **B2.** ten · **B3.** Related · **B4.** H1
</details>

<details><summary><strong>Part C — grading notes</strong></summary>

Grading looks for: intent labels that match the phrase (not guessed), SERP evidence for the pick (small focused sites/forums = winnable; big brands = go more specific), and a complete set obeying the length rules (≤60 title, ~150 description). H2s must follow a *logical order* (e.g., choose → prepare → plant → care → harvest).
</details>

<details><summary><strong>Part D</strong></summary>

**D1.** Bug: keyword stuffing — repeating the keyword unnaturally. Fix: phrase in title, H1, and a few natural uses; write for the reader; one clean title ≤60 chars. · **D2.** Bug: head terms ("tomatoes", "insurance") are unwinnable for new sites. Fix: long-tail first, win, expand outward. · **D3.** (bonus) Google believes the page best matches fertilizer-intent: check whether the content/description actually targets the main keyword, or accept the win and align the page with what's ranking (intent beats wishes).
</details>

<details><summary><strong>Part E — what a strong answer includes</strong></summary>

Research first: autocomplete/PAA/Related for bakery + city terms; two realistic long-tail pages (e.g., "custom birthday cakes in [city]", "gluten-free bakery [city]") each with its own page; patience: new pages take weeks-to-months, so publish consistently and compound — starting early is the whole game.
</details>
