You can export the entire workspace first, then create a separate blank workspace.

### 1. Export your current workspace

Open Settings in the sidebar.

Under Workspace, select General.

Choose Export all workspace content.

Wait for Notion’s email or in-app notification with the download link.

Download the archive and keep a backup copy.

The export includes your pages as HTML, Markdown, and databases as CSV, along with uploaded files. A sitemap is included to help navigate the HTML/Markdown export. Business and Enterprise plans may also offer a workspace-wide PDF export. ⁠[](https://www.notion.so/help/export-your-content)⁠​

Keep in mind:

Exporting can take up to 30 hours for a large workspace.

The download link expires after 7 days.

Pages you cannot access—such as other users’ private pages—may not be included.

Some teamspace content may be excluded based on workspace settings.

### 2. Create a brand-new workspace

Click your current workspace name at the top of the sidebar.

Choose the option to create a new workspace.

Give it a new name and complete the setup.

Switch between the old and new workspaces from the same workspace menu.

I recommend keeping the old workspace intact until you have downloaded the archive and verified that important pages, databases, and attachments are present. If you want to migrate selected content into the new workspace later, you can import Markdown, CSV, HTML, or other supported formats.

### 3. Delete the old workspace only if you’re sure

After verifying the archive, go to:

Settings → Workspace → General → Danger zone → Delete entire workspace

Treat deletion as permanent: it removes the workspace content and access for everyone in it. Export first, and do not delete the old workspace if you may need its permissions, comments, history, or database structure later. ⁠[](https://www.notion.so/help/delete-a-workspace)⁠
