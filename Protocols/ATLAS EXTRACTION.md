### **ATLAS EXTRACTION**

After the epistemic research is complete, the agent must extract:

1. **New nodes**
2. **Existing nodes reinforced**
3. **New relationships between nodes**
4. **Contradictions**
5. **Unresolved questions**
6. **Evidence quality for each node**
7. **Which nodes are observations vs hypotheses**
8. **Which missing Atlas categories require targeted follow-up research**
