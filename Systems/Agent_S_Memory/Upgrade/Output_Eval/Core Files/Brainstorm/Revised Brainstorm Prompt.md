Revised Brainstorm Prompt**

Use this as your stronger brainstorm prompt:

You are generating candidate ideas for a serious selection process, not for casual inspiration.

Mission:  
Produce a ranked list of 8 app or system ideas that are strong enough to survive later evaluation.

Goal:  
Find ideas that feel native to the 2026 landscape, exploit real structural gaps, and create immediate strategic interest.

Hard rules:

- no generic “AI for X” ideas
- no implementation steps
- no vague trend summaries
- no ideas that would have sounded equally plausible in 2020
- no fluff
- no safety-padding language
- if an idea is weak, do not include it

Selection criteria:  
Every idea must be:

- ambitious
- specific
- grounded in a real 2026 shift
- clear enough to evaluate
- strategically interesting
- feasible at very low or zero production cost if that requirement is active

For each idea, use this structure:

1. Idea Name
2. Core Thesis  
    A one-sentence statement of what the product is and why it matters.
3. Problem  
    What exact unmet need, friction, asymmetry, or structural gap exists?
4. Solution  
    What is the core mechanism or product move?
5. Why Now  
    What changed in 2026 that makes this newly viable or urgent?
6. Strategic Edge  
    Why is this more than a generic product idea?  
    What makes it structurally strong?
7. Zero-Cost Lever  
    How can this plausibly be built or launched with open-source tools, free tiers, existing subscriptions, or low-cost infrastructure?

Final section:  
After generating all 8 ideas, add:

- Top 3 by raw strategic potential
- One sentence on the main risk for each Top 3 idea
- One sentence naming the single most build-worthy candidate right now
